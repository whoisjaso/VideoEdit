# Edit Scorecard: score every talking-head edit before it ships

Jason (2026-10-10): "let's break down a video into several categories, psychology included, so we know the area we need revision."

**How to use it**
- Score each category 0–10 against its checks.
- The total is the weighted average.
- Fill it in from the 1 fps review sheet of the FINAL render (not from the plan) before sending. Send it along with the video.
- Any category under 7 is fixed before delivery.
- When Jason scores the video, log both his score and this one in `journal.md`. If they disagree, the weights are wrong: adjust them.

| # | Category | Weight | What 10/10 looks like (measurable checks) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook (0–3 s)** | 15% | **Verbal:** his boldest line. **Visual:** a full-screen designed frame by frame 15 (≤6 elements, from→to or proof). Something moves on frame 0. Viewer knows within 3 s why to stay. |
| 2 | **Psychology** | 15% | **Viewer's want:** named early (what *they* get, not just what he is). **Curiosity:** an open loop closed at the payoff. **Identity:** he speaks to who the viewer wants to be. **"You" turn:** at least one line aimed at the viewer. **Proof before claim.** **Emotion:** an arc of tension → release → conviction. **Pattern interrupt:** every 6–8 s. |
| 3 | **Story & structure** | 10% | **One idea**, said in a sentence. Hook → problem/tension → argument → payoff → CTA. **No repeated lines:** cut retakes that say the same thing twice. **Callback:** the end answers the opening. |
| 4 | **Retention & pacing** | 15% | **Visual change** at least every 2–3 s. **No bare-face stretch over 4 s** (captions alone don't count). **Bare-face share** ≤ 25% of runtime. **Length** fits the density (≤ 1:30 unless every section earns it). |
| 5 | **Motion-graphics coverage** | 10% | **One designed beat per sentence** (§18 bar). **Literal:** each visual means the words. **Change while on screen:** no single graphic sits unchanged > 4 s. |
| 6 | **Motion craft** | 10% | **Smooth** 30 fps with motion blur. **Springs:** critically damped. **Timing:** every entrance on a spoken word. **Exits:** clean, no pops, no overlaps. |
| 7 | **Visual language** | 10% | **One world:** one look across face and cutaways. **Type:** ≤ 2 type families. **Colour:** one accent with a fixed meaning. **No brightness whiplash:** dark car → white page needs a bridge. |
| 8 | **Captions** | 5% | **Size & place:** readable on a phone, clear of the face and the graphics. **Gold** ≈ 8% of words. **Never twice:** a word never appears in both a caption and a graphic. **Movement:** captions always move. |
| 9 | **Sound** | 5% | **Voice:** clean and up front at −14 LUFS. **Sync:** every graphic has its sound, peaks within 1–2 frames. **Variety:** impacts on the lines that land, real iOS UI sounds. **Silence:** no dead stretches. |
| 10 | **Accuracy & ending** | 5% | **Words:** exactly his, faith lines confirmed. **CTA:** a real offer or reason to follow. **Ending:** a clean last frame, no abrupt cut. |

**Total** = Σ score × weight. The bar to beat is **8.3** (episode #3).
