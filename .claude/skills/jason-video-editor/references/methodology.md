## 1. Psychology & premise
- **Value first, then the ask.** 9/12 teach or give something. The ask is small and specific: **comment a keyword** to get a free thing in DMs [r01 r03 r04 r08 r09 r10 r11], **save this video** [r12], "screenshot this" [r03]. It turns attention into engagement (comments and saves push reach) and into leads.
- **Show the result before the method.** [r05] opens on the finished animation, [r09] shows the finished edit next to its timeline, [r12] plays before/after audio first. The viewer wants the result, so they stay for the how (same open-loop rule as our cold open).
- **Proof by pattern.** [r02]: not one example but three (MrBeast, then again, then Hormozi), then "coincidence?" The viewer convinces themselves before the answer.
- **Name the concept.** [r03] "something called a **power word**", [r02] "colors **120° apart**". A named idea feels like insider knowledge and is easy to remember and repeat.
- **"The answer / reason is simple"** [r02 r03] right before the payoff: it promises low effort, so attention spikes at the key line.

## 2. Hooks (first 1-3 seconds)
Five hook shapes in the set; pick the one that fits the episode:
1. **Question the viewer can't answer yet**: "Do you notice the common color theme across all of MrBeast's thumbnails?" [r02]
2. **Contrast**: "This hook is okay, but this hook is more scroll-stopping." [r03] (show both)
3. **"What if I told you…"** + an impossible-sounding result [r05]
4. **Call out their pain**: "Still cutting your audio like this?" [r12], "Your edits feel flat?" [r09]
5. **Want + offer**: "Want these premium sound effects?" [r01 r10]
Rules: text on screen by frame 1-3; the hook words themselves appear **word by word** in sync with the voice [r03 r12]; the visual proves the hook within ~1s (the mockup, the before/after, the thumbnail grid).

## 3. Typography system
- **Sandwich hierarchy** [r01 r10 r12]: small light/italic setup → **one BIG bold accent word with glow** → small closer. One accent word per title.
- **One word at a time, synced to speech** [r03 r06 r12]: each word ~0.2-0.3s, centered high; on the key phrase, words **grow** as they stack (more < scroll < **STOPPING**) [r03].
- **Karaoke captions** for screen-led teaching [r02]: small white bold, low-center; the phrase builds word by word and clears at the phrase end. Captions stay quiet when the screen is the star.
- **Two-tier caption** "for **ZOOMS**" [r08] = our locked yellow two-tier style. Confirmed again.
- **Keyword highlight box** (yellow/gold block behind one word) [r03], and **quote marks** around a named concept ("Hidden", "SFX") [r01 r03 r09 r10].
- **Blinking caret** after a single keyword ("action. |") [r06]: it reads as typed thought.
- **Persistent header label** for the whole video [r05] ("Claude is insane!! ✳"), or a persistent CTA frame (top "Comment "SFX"", bottom "Get the Free Pack") [r09]: the topic and the offer never leave the screen.
- Fonts in the set: SF/Inter-style bold sans (most), condensed bold (r07), **serif bold italic** for the premium CTA [r04 r09], Nunito-style rounded heavy for neon titles [r10]. Never more than 2 families per video.
- Entrances: **blur-in from a slight offset**, ~6 frames, staggered word by word [r01 r03 r10 r12]. Exits: blur/fade, never a hard cut, except the full-screen slam.

## 4. Color & light
- **One accent color per video** on a neutral or dark base: red [r01], green [r10], gold [r04 r08 r12], orange/red [r05], blue [r11].
- **Triadic contrast** [r02]: colors ~120° apart on the wheel pop hardest (red+blue, yellow+blue, orange+green, purple+green, yellow+red). Use for thumbnails, the hero word vs the background, and the two sides of a comparison.
- **Light↔dark flip on a new concept** [r03 r06]: the background inverts when the idea changes. A visual "new chapter" with no label needed.
- **Full-screen color slam** [r05 "CRAZY." on orange, r06 red frame]: 10-20 frames, once or twice per video, on the punchline.
- Glow on the accent only (text-shadow in the accent color); everything else flat.

## 5. Motion & layout
- **Something is always moving** [r03 r05 r12]: a slow camera drift or scale on every frame, even on still mockups.
- **Mockups as the B-roll**: iPhone/Reels UI [r03], an NLE timeline with a playhead [r01 r09 r10 r11], a laptop/monitor filmed at an angle [r02 r05], floating tilted 3D panels [r12]. (= our locked iOS-coded motion graphics, extended: real app/editor UIs are the visual language.)
- **Comparison by scale**: the winner scales up to center, the loser shrinks away [r03].
- **Assemble → scatter** [r06]: objects gather into a shape on the idea, then explode on the turn.
- **Playhead metaphor** [r01 r04 r10 r11]: a fixed center line with content scrolling past. Use for timelines, sequences, "step by step".

## 6. SFX ↔ animation (when to put sound, measured)
- **Sound lands ON the motion, or leads it by up to 2 frames. Never late.** Measured audio-minus-visual: r05 −83 ms, r06 −70 ms, r09 −33 ms, r12 −33 ms, r08 +28 ms, r02 +3 ms, r03 −45 ms. Use `<Hit>` (lands the peak/onset on the frame); for whooshes into a cut, put the hit 1-2 frames before.
- **Canonical pairings** [r08, matching playbook §3 ×N]: Woosh → every zoom; Pop + Click → every pop-up; Riser → suspense (ends ON the payoff); Camera Shutter → section transitions; Magic Reveal / sparkle → big reveals.
- **One sound per visual event, and every event gets one** in screen/mockup-led videos [r09 r12]: typing per word on kinetic type, click per UI element, whoosh per move. Talking-head sections stay sparser (voice first).
- **Name sounds by job** [r10 r11 r08]: our library does this (`references/sfx-library.md`).
- **Layer on tracks** [r09]: separate lanes for impacts, whooshes, UI, ambience so levels can be balanced.

## 7. Audio craft
- **No dead stops** [r12]: when the voice is cut at a section end, add a short **echo/reverb tail** (duplicate the clip's last word, reverb only, fade ~0.4-0.8s) so the cut never sounds chopped. (Fixes the class of problem Jason heard in ep #4 v1.)
- Voice is king: SFX sit 6-12 dB under the voice; the only exceptions are full-screen slams with no speech under them.

## 8. Format & CTA
- **Comment-keyword CTA** is the dominant close [7/12]: big keyword in quotes, glowing ("Comment "SFX""), plus what they get ("I'll send it to you / in your DM"). Show it **from the middle onward** as a persistent element in offer videos [r01 r09 r10], or at the end in story videos.
- Save-bait for list/tutorial content: "save this video!" / "screenshot" [r03 r12].
