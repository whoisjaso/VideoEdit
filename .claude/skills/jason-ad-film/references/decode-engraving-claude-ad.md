## The techniques that make it look expensive
1. **One visual language.** Every asset is a grayscale engraving, plate or plaster photo, cut out or printed on white. No colour photos and no rounded cards.
2. **One accent with one job.** Orange is only the dot, the spark and punctuation. It covers about 1–5% of pixels.
3. **The dot is the edit.** Every cut is a match cut on the dot's position, so the eye never searches.
4. **Construction geometry** (compass circles, arcs, ruled lines, letter-construction grids) is the "paint on top". It is thin black hairlines, never decoration for its own sake.
5. **Fast:**
   - shots are 0.13–0.5 s;
   - swaps inside a fixed frame every 2–4 frames read as one shot that is "thinking";
   - 2 black flash frames give polarity resets.
6. **Type is one word, bold lowercase serif.** Morphs change one letter (draft→craft) or weight (th ink→think).
7. **Bookended:** it opens on the lockup and closes on the lockup.
8. **3D used once:** a soft clay spark with a contact shadow, never a template card-flip.
