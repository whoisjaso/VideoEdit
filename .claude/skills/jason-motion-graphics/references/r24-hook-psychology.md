# r24: HOOK PSYCHOLOGY. Retention for short-form (Jason, 2026-10-12: "this is the psychology for short form content/hooks")
**Source:** a 22 s creator reel. The speaker talks to camera at his desk. Pop-in cards show Instagram's "How long people watched your reel" graph: a magenta curve on dark iOS cards. A segment timeline on a light grid paper builds `Hook | Question/Delay #1 | Rehook | Question/Delay #2 | Answer`.

**Transcript:**
> If your graph looks like this, here's the easiest way to get it looking like this. And 90% of you get this completely wrong. Your hook was fine and you teased something that people wanted to know, but then you immediately answered it. They had no reason to stay. If you're gonna answer your hook, first ask another question or delay the solution. Throw in a rehook to remind them what they're watching for, or even give them a second reason to stay. And when you finally reveal the payoff, immediately end the video.

## The rule (every short-form script and edit)
1. **The hook opens a loop:** tease the thing they want to know. Show the result first ("graph like this → like this").
2. **Never answer the hook straight away.** Answering it is where the curve cliffs (his red dot on the drop).
3. **Delay #1:** ask another question, or give the context needed *before* the answer.
4. **Rehook at about the midpoint:** remind them what they're waiting for ("so how did he…?"), or give a second reason to stay (a bigger reveal is coming).
5. **Delay #2:** one more step, then the **payoff**.
6. **End right after the payoff.** No outro, recap or "so yeah". The CTA rides inside the payoff line or the last 1–2 s.

**Script template:** `HOOK (0–3 s) → DELAY 1 → REHOOK (~40–50%) → DELAY 2 → PAYOFF → cut`. Mark the timestamps of all five in every short-form plan.

## Visual language of the reference (reusable)
- Graph cards: dark iOS rounded card, magenta curve drawing on, a green dot when it holds, a red dot and a shaded band on the drop, a yellow "Payoff" tag.
- Segment timeline building block by block on a light grid paper: yellow Hook, blue Question/Delay, orange Rehook (with a ↓ marker), purple Delay #2, green Answer. Each block pops in with `back`.
- The speaker is half-frame under the graphic. Captions are one bold white word at a time, on the line.
- His face cuts back in full-screen between the graphic beats.
