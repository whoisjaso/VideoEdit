# r27: COLOR TRIAD. Thumbnail and cover colour psychology (Jason, 2026-10-12: "color references")
**Source:** a 33 s reel. Points at MrBeast's and Alex Hormozi's YouTube grids next to a colour wheel and a ChatGPT answer, then the creator talks to camera with a card of pairs.

**Claim:** colours **120° apart** on the wheel (a triad, "one third across") give the highest visual contrast. That makes them more eye-catching in a scrolling feed, which gives a clearer hierarchy, faster reading, and better click-through.
- MrBeast: **red + blue**, **yellow + blue**.
- Hormozi: **yellow + blue** (blue cards, yellow numbers and words).

## Approved pairs (the reference card)
| Pair | Use |
|---|---|
| **Red + Blue** | Conflict, stakes, versus |
| **Yellow + Blue** | Money, numbers, "how to" (the Hormozi look) |
| **Yellow + Red** | Urgency, warning, hype |
| **Orange + Green** | Growth, health, food |
| **Purple + Green** | Mystery, premium, spiritual |

## The rule
- **Thumbnails, covers, title cards and hook frames:** build the frame on **one triad pair**.
  - One colour is the field (background or light).
  - The other is the subject accent (text, outline, prop, glow).
  - Everything else is neutral (skin, black, white).
- **Pick the pair by the video's emotion** (table above), then keep it across the series so the grid reads as a brand.
- **Inside the edit:** the meaning colours still hold (gold = key idea, green = money/wins, red = problems). Grade so the two pair colours stay the most saturated things on screen.
- **Check it:** shrink the thumbnail to 160 px wide next to 5 real thumbnails from the niche. If it doesn't pop first, push the pair's saturation and separation.
