## The first hour

- Every reply is answered inside the hour. Match the commenter's pace. Answer the question they asked. Where there is a gap in their words, one question back.
- The angry comments go first, and get the calmest answers. They are who the film is for.
- Nobody gets argued with. The question to ask: "What did your brain run when you read the hook?"
- Never reply with a link. Never reply with "watch the video."
- If someone cites the IAT's limits, agree. It measures pairing speed, and it predicts one person's behaviour poorly. The film only claims the pairing. SOURCES.md has the references.
- If someone says "not every race shows it": they are right about the averages. Black test-takers show no preference on average (Nosek et al., 2007). The film says it shows up in people of every race, which is true of individuals.

## What not to do

- No engagement bait ("agree?"). No "controversial opinion."
- Never post the hook without its second line. "So is mine" is the whole frame; without it the post is just the word.
- Never use "racism is good." It is not the claim.
