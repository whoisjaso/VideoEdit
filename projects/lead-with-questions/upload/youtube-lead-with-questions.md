### Description
```
Fall asleep rehearsing the conversation you want tomorrow: you ask, they answer, you let the silence work.

This is a 28-minute guided sleep session for men who sell: on a car lot, to business owners, to investors, in boardrooms, and in interviews. Slow breathing, a body scan, a countdown, a short story, identity lines in plain English, and five rehearsal scenes. Then it fades into silence so sleep can do its normal job with what you practiced.

The rule: I lead with questions. The person asking the questions holds the frame.

WHAT THIS IS
Spoken affirmations and guided mental rehearsal for leading conversations with honest questions, timed for falling asleep (night) and waking up (morning). What it isn't: magic, medicine, or a hidden message. The soft tones under the music are optional binaural beats (headphones only); research on them is mixed and modest. The music is tuned to 432 Hz for sound, not because 432 Hz has proven special effects. A quiet whisper layer repeats the same lines you can already hear; the full text is below. Truly inaudible "subliminal" messages have little or no proven effect, so nothing here depends on them. Sources: Greenwald et al. 1991; Ingendoh et al. 2023; Garcia-Argibay et al. 2019; Calamassi & Pomponi 2019; Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006; Toth et al. 2020; Hu et al. 2020; Kross et al. 2014. Don't listen while driving. Not medical advice.

WHAT YOU'RE PRACTICING
• Open with a question, not a pitch
• One question at a time
• Ask, then stay quiet until they answer
• Say it back in one line, in their words
• Say the price once, clean, then ask or go quiet
• End on one specific next step
• No guilt, no fake deadlines, no made-up proof

HOW TO USE IT (30 days)
In bed, lights off, phone face down or locked. Headphones for the binaural tones, or the speaker version on a nightstand. Keep the volume low, just loud enough to follow the words. Press play as the last thing before sleep. Use a sleep timer or auto-lock; don't loop it all night. Pair it with the Morning Run when you wake up.
After every big conversation, take 30 seconds in your notes app: Did I open with a question? Questions vs. statements? Did I hold the silence? Did they say the problem in their own words? Did I say the price once, clean? Did it end on one next step? Compare week 1 to week 4. If nothing changes, that's useful information too.

HOW IT SOUNDS
Original score in F major at 60 BPM (felt piano, warm pad, cello drone), tuned to A = 432 Hz. This is a sound choice only; 432 Hz has no proven special effect (one small pilot study, not replicated). Soft rain and brown noise, there for comfort and masking only. Wave swells follow the guided breaths, and a singing bowl sounds under each anchor. One soft two-note "question tone" plays after every key question, then a real silence: question, tone, silence. Three faint repeats of that tone play after the voice ends, as an experimental cue (Hu et al. 2020, lab-based). Binaural beats glide from 10 Hz down to 4 Hz on headphones; the evidence they change brain state is mixed and modest. A speaker version uses gentle isochronic tones instead, and the research on those is even thinner.

SAFETY
```
