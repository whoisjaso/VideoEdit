**Description**

```
Say it. Do it. No pump fakes.

God named Himself I AM. Not I was. Not I will be. This session puts that name to work in you, at the one moment your mind stops arguing: right as you fall asleep.

Six rounds of Wim Hof breathing. Every out-breath carries a line you're about to live by. Every empty-lung hold drops you into a scene, happening now, not someday. Then the body slows, and you fall asleep inside who you already are.

The law of this session:
God is not a man, that He should lie. He binds Himself to His word. You're made in His image, so your word creates too, but only while you keep it. No bluffing. No pump fakes. Word over feelings.

What you're installing:
• My word is law: I say it, I do it
• The true I AM over the ego mask: creator, builder, the one who puts people in better positions
• Living in the present: the past is where depression lives, the future is where anxiety lives
• Confidence: slow speech, steady eyes, a firm handshake, nobody rushes me
• Persistence over intensity: one haircut doesn't make a barber
• Intelligent, charismatic, a gentleman, never timid, never compromising with unfairness

How it sounds:
The music is tuned to 432 Hz, with a soft binaural pulse at theta speed under it (headphones). In every round the music falls away and your breath takes over. Ticks and a chime count you into every round and out of every hold.
```
