# I AM shorts: posting kit

Files: `renders/shorts/short-<name>.mp4` (1080x1920, 30 fps). Voice + SFX only; add the track in-app.

**Track picks** (from the playbook's music-by-mood list):
- Men's clips: *Experience* (Einaudi). Swap in *I Wanna Be Yours* (Arctic Monkeys) for the confrontational ones (pump-faker, not-humble).
- Women's clips: *comet* (Frank Saint) or *Experience*.

Keep the track low (about 15–20%) so the voice wins.

**Posting:** one a day, staggered by gender. Pin the full-video link (YouTube Shorts: related video = the full session).
