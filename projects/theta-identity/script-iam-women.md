# I AM: the script (women)

A second session, with its own look and sound (forest green and gold). Same engine as `script.md`.

Rules for this one, on top of the usual (first person, plain words, identity not ego):

1. **Present tense, everywhere.** No "future self", no "one day", no "I remember". The past is
   where depression lives and the future is where anxiety lives. I AM lives now. Scenes play as
   *now*, and close with "This is me."
2. **The Word.** I am made in the image of God, who creates by His Word and keeps it. My word
   creates too, but only while I keep it. No bluffing. No pump fakes.
3. **Identity is repetition.** Not intensity. Persistence.

Build: `python projects/theta-identity/build.py --script script-iam-women.md --name iam-women-v1`
