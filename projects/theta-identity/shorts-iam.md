# I AM: short-form clips

Vertical 9:16 clips that sell the full I AM sessions. Built by `shorts.py`:

    python projects/theta-identity/shorts.py shorts-iam.md

Format:
- `## name` starts a short.
- `voice:` sets the Kokoro voice, `speed:` the delivery.
- `hook:` is the full-screen text slam over the first line. Mark words `^gold`, `+green` (wins) or `!red` (problems).
- `> line` is spoken, with captions word by word. Use the same marks, rationed to about one per idea.
- `cta:` is the end card's small line.

Hooks are call-outs aimed at the viewer ("you're a pump faker"), never at a group of people.
