# Plutarch's Lives: a 2-hour 16:9 belief film (sleep-pace)
Jason, 2026-10-11. A visual retelling of Plutarch's Lives in modern English. The lens: beliefs formed unconsciously → identity → action ("who you are when no one is looking"). It's made to watch while drifting to sleep; short-form hooks get cut from it afterwards.

## Locked decisions
- **Format:** 16:9, about 2 h, built in chapters (one Life ≈ 18–20 min).
- **Pilot:** Alexander the Great. Napoleon appears as the cold open: he grew up reading Plutarch, which is the thesis in action.
- **Voices (Kokoro, free):** two narrators at sleep pace (speed 0.82, 1.1 s between lines, 2.2 s when the voice changes).
  - `bm_george` tells the history.
  - `bm_fable` is the inner voice: it names the belief and gives a MODERN example so the viewer can picture it.
- **Images:**
  - public-domain art first (Met CC0 statues, engravings, paintings);
  - Higgsfield (credits approved) for scenes no painting covers.
- **Source:** the public-domain Dryden/Clough translation (Gutenberg #674, `src/` not committed). Jason will also send his own copy for analysis.
  - The narration is always our own modern retelling, never the book's wording.
  - The psychology lens is labelled as interpretation, never put in Plutarch's mouth.
- **Delivery:** multi-GB files go to a destination Jason picks, not chat or Git LFS.

## Each Life (structure)
1. The story (chronological, cinematic) — George
2. The belief he formed, and where it came from — Fable, with a modern example
3. His daily practices — both
4. Who he was when no one was looking — Fable
5. The lesson, in one sentence — George
