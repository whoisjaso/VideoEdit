## Description
Julius Caesar once put down a book about Alexander the Great and cried. "At my age, Alexander was already king of so many nations, and I have done nothing great."

This is not entertainment. It's an investment.

Plutarch set Alexander and Caesar side by side so we would read them together. Last time you lived as Alexander. Tonight you won't watch Caesar. You'll stand where he stood:
• the pirates' camp, where the room said "prisoner" and he answered "Caesar";
• the bank of the Rubicon, where thinking ends and one act changes everything;
• the storm, where confidence moves men but not the sea;
• and the morning of the Ides, holding a warning he never read.

Put it on as you drift off. Let the mind slow down. Listen not as a spectator. As them.
