# Prologue: before you sleep (opens every chapter)

**Voices:** GUIDE = bf_emma (warm, unhurried). Same speed and pauses as the chapters.

**Source:** Jason's framework (`../framework.md`): the Observer, identity as evidence, ego as a rented identity, "who are we, here?", and bedtime stories.

**Science, stated carefully:**
- **Sleep stages.** As you fall asleep, the brain shifts from beta (alert waking) to alpha (relaxed) and then theta (the hypnagogic edge of sleep). It is not beta.
- **Mental rehearsal.** Imagining an action engages much of the same brain machinery as doing it. That's what mental-rehearsal research shows. We don't claim the brain literally can't tell the two apart.

### Prologue
[▸ Darkness. One candle. Slow plates: a sleeper by candlelight; an eye; Napoleon; Alexander; the book under the pillow.]

**GUIDE:** Before we begin, you should know why this was made. It isn't entertainment. It's an investment. In you.

**GUIDE:** Everything you do comes from what you believe about yourself. Not what you say you believe. What a quieter part of you believes. Call it the Observer. It watches everything you do, and it keeps the record. It doesn't listen to your words. It believes your actions.

**GUIDE:** From that record, it answers one question, in every room you walk into: who are we, here? Whatever it answers, you become. Your voice, your posture, your decisions follow.

**GUIDE:** There's a second voice: the ego. It's the identity you borrow from other people, from the way you think they see you. It feels real, but it's rented. It changes with the room. Walk out, and it's gone.

**GUIDE:** Identity is different. It's built from evidence. And the strongest evidence is gathered where no one is watching. Only in front of God, and yourself.

**GUIDE:** So why stories? Why Alexander, and Napoleon? Because before Napoleon ever led an army, he spent his nights reading about men like Alexander. And Alexander slept with the story of Achilles under his pillow. The greatest people who ever lived were shaped by the stories they let in.

**GUIDE:** And when you let them in matters. As you drift toward sleep, your brain slows down. The guarded, busy waking mind gives way to slower waves. First alpha. Then theta, the borderland of dreams. Here, pictures come easily, and the mind is open.

**GUIDE:** Your brain uses much of the same machinery to imagine an action as it does to perform it. So tonight, you won't just hear what they did. You'll stand where they stood. You'll make their choices. And somewhere in the night, the Observer files a quiet note: we have been here before. We know how this is done.

**GUIDE:** That isn't the whole work. A belief becomes who you are only when you act on it, again and again. But this is the first step. The picture. The spark. The first impulse to move.

**GUIDE:** So get comfortable. Let your breathing slow. And listen. Not as a spectator. As them.
